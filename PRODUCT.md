# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are developers, evaluators, and prospective adopters who do not own a physical robot but need to understand and experience Injenium's skill market. They arrive curious but may be cautious about wallets, unfamiliar network terminology, and irreversible transactions. Existing robot operators are a secondary audience who need the same interface as a transparent control and verification surface.

## Product Purpose

Provide a complete browser-based path from discovering a robot skill, validating and loading its capability package, running a safe simulation, and experiencing the market flow on a local sandbox, Injective testnet, or Injective mainnet. Success means a new user can understand the project through action, complete a risk-free first run without hardware, and deliberately graduate to signed chain transactions without exposing private keys to the application.

## Positioning

Injenium makes robot capabilities inspectable, safely loadable, and economically testable before physical hardware or irreversible value is involved.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, direct, and trustworthy. The interface should lower Web3 anxiety with plain language and progressive disclosure while retaining the precision expected from a chain-backed developer tool.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a promotional Web3 landing page or a generic enterprise administration dashboard. Avoid speculative trading aesthetics, inflated marketing claims, decorative crypto motifs, dense configuration-first screens, and unexplained protocol jargon.

## Design Principles

- Let users experience the product before asking them to connect a wallet.
- Make trust inspectable through permissions, hashes, network identity, and transaction previews.
- Separate simulation from settlement so irreversible actions are always explicit.
- Explain chain concepts at the moment they matter, using plain language.
- Keep every state recoverable or clearly traceable, especially pending and failed transactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, color-blind-safe status communication, readable contrast, semantic landmarks and labels, and reduced-motion preferences. Error and risk states must never rely on color alone.
